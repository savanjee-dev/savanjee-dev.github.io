Drishyam.
A little wonder, every day.

A photo discovery experience built around slowing down and seeing the world differently. Drishyam brings photography, visual stories and thoughtful mobile interactions into one immersive Android app.

01 / The idea

Photography, without the rush.

Drishyam is a visual discovery app for people who enjoy photography. Its experience centres on a flowing image feed, browsable themes and a focused viewer that gives each photograph room to breathe. The app has its own moss-and-olive visual identity, expressive typography and carefully layered surfaces.

The aim is simple: make discovering photographs feel calm, immersive and personal — while keeping useful details such as creator credits, source information and image actions close at hand.
