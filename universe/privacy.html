Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Universe handles information when you visit this website.

Information collected

Universe is currently a static discovery website. We do not ask visitors to create accounts, and we do not intentionally collect personal information through the homepage. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may process technical data such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and secure the website.

External services and links

Pages may load resources from third-party providers (for example, Google Fonts and image providers) and link to streaming or discovery services. Those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies. When you leave Universe, please review the privacy policy of the destination website.

Advertising and analytics

Universe does not currently display Google AdSense ads or use website analytics. If advertising, analytics, or other tracking tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain relevant data collection, cookies and choices before or when those features are introduced.

Cookies

Universe does not intentionally set its own advertising or analytics cookies at present. Third-party resources or linked websites may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Children

This website is a general entertainment discovery guide and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website changes. Please check this page for the latest version.
