About Universe

Universe is an independent movie and series discovery guide designed to help viewers explore entertainment and find official streaming destinations in India.

What we do

We publish discovery ideas, watch guides and links to official streaming services. Universe does not host, upload, distribute or stream films or television episodes. Playback, subscriptions, pricing, language options and regional availability are managed by the respective services.

Editorial approach

We aim to make our recommendations useful, clear and honest. Release dates and streaming availability can change, so readers should verify details on the official platform before subscribing, renting or purchasing. If you spot an error, please let us know.

Independence

Universe is an independent guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, SonyLIV, ZEE5 or other services mentioned on this site. Their names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
